1. Refund Policy

SIERRAUNLOCK operates on a strict no-refund policy after payment confirmation, except in cases of proven fraud or service failure. This policy exists because:

  • Cryptocurrency payments (Binance, Bitcoin, Ethereum) are irreversible by nature. Once sent, they cannot be reversed by the sender or recipient.
  • Orange Money transactions are final and cannot be recalled after completion.
  • Cash payments are physically exchanged and cannot be reversed.
  • Upstream unlock services (from FastUnlockers) are consumed immediately upon order placement. The wholesale cost is deducted from our reseller account balance the moment we place the order.

⚠️ Important: Once Paid, Orders Cannot Be Reversed

After you complete payment (via Orange Money, Binance Pay, or cash), the order enters our processing queue. At this point, the transaction is final and irreversible. Customers cannot cancel or reverse payments through their payment provider.

Exceptions: When Refunds ARE Possible

Refunds are founder-only decisions made after fraud review. We may issue refunds in these specific cases:

  • Service failure: The upstream unlock service fails to deliver a working code within the stated timeframe (e.g., "1-2 hours" but takes 7 days with no result).
  • Wrong service ordered: Customer ordered the wrong service (e.g., AT&T unlock for a T-Mobile phone) AND the order has NOT yet been placed upstream.
  • Duplicate payment: Customer accidentally paid twice for the same job ID.
  • Proven fraud: Customer provides false information or attempts to scam the system.

5. Blacklist & Stolen Device Policy

⚠️ We NEVER Unlock Blacklisted or Stolen Devices

SIERRAUNLOCK has a zero-tolerance policy for unlocking devices reported as:

  • Blacklisted: Reported to carriers as lost, stolen, or involved in fraud
  • Stolen: Reported to police or insurance companies
  • Insurance claims: Devices with active insurance claims
  • Financed devices: Devices still under payment plans (unless paid off and verified)

How We Detect Blacklisted Devices

Before processing any unlock, we run an IMEI check against:

  • GSMA global blacklist database
  • Carrier-specific blacklists (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, etc.)
  • Insurance claim databases

If your device is blacklisted, we will:

  • Refuse the unlock request
  • Issue a full refund (since no upstream cost was incurred)
  • Advise you to contact the carrier or police to resolve the blacklist status
