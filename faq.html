Unlocking a device allows consumers to move it from one network to another compatible network. The process varies by device and provider — a provider may unlock automatically after certain conditions, send instructions on request, or complete it in-store. Some phones are sold with built-in software that prevents them from working on a different carrier. Unlocking removes those technical limitations, although it does not resolve every technological compatibility issue between networks.
