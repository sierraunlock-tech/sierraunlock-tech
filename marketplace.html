Seller Rules (read before posting)

  • Photo of the actual item is mandatory — compressed automatically and shown on your listing.
  • State exactly what it fits (e.g. "iPhone 11", "all Tecno Spark series", "HP EliteBook 840 G5").
  • Second-hand items must disclose every defect and be tested in front of the buyer.
  • Proof of ownership required. Stolen goods = ban + police report.
  • Misrepresentation = permanent ban and public notice in our WhatsApp channel.

⚖️ Submitting means you accept the Seller Rules and ID verification. Your photo is compressed on your device and sent with your listing for admin approval. Also attach the same photo in the WhatsApp chat that opens.
