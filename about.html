About SIERRAUNLOCK

Built on trust, engineered for reliability. We are Sierra Leone's first dedicated digital unlocking and repair platform, bridging the gap between local technicians and global unlocking servers.

⚠️ IMEI / blacklist tampering is ILLEGAL. SIERRAUNLOCK performs only legal carrier & network unlocks in line with CTIA consumer standards and Sierra Leone law. Proof of ownership is required.

Our Core Philosophy

The two pillars that guide every repair, unlock, and business transaction we handle.

It Can Only Be God

We approach every complex board-level repair and difficult network unlock with humility and dedication. When human limits are reached, we rely on faith, patience, and precision to deliver results for our customers.

Learn Before You Earn

Technology evolves daily. Before we charge a client, we ensure our engineers have mastered the tool, the firmware, and the hardware. Continuous education is mandatory for every SIERRAUNLOCK technician.
